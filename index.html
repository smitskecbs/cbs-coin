Our story

CBS started as a community token built around transparency, collaboration, and sovereignty — the idea that value and direction should come from the people who show up, not from closed rooms or hidden agendas.

What began as a small experiment on Solana has grown through learning, testing, and building in public. CBS remains the community layer of the project; CBS Tools is where that energy turns into practical, open software for everyone on Solana.

Free, open tools for creating, launching, and managing Solana projects. CBS Tools is the main development focus of the ecosystem.

Mobile game · CBS ecosystem

CBS-GO

CBS-GO is a mobile web app where you can walk around, open loot stars, collect tickets and CBS Play, and get introduced to the CBS ecosystem in a more playful way.

If you are new to crypto, CBS-GO makes it easier to start by creating a local wallet on your phone and guiding you through the basics. If you already know Solana, it gives you a new way to explore rewards, wallet features and community mechanics inside the CBS ecosystem.

CBS is a community-driven Solana project focused on building tools, experiments and rewards around its own ecosystem.

New to crypto?

CBS-GO is built to lower the barrier. You can start on your phone, learn the basics step by step and explore the app at your own pace.
