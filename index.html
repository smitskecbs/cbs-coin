Our story

CBS started as a community token built around transparency, collaboration, and sovereignty — the idea that value and direction should come from the people who show up, not from closed rooms or hidden agendas.

What began as a small experiment on Solana has grown through learning, testing, and building in public. CBS remains the community layer of the project; CBS Tools is where that energy turns into practical, open software for everyone on Solana.

CBS Access Pass

CBS Access Pass is an experimental holder pass for future ecosystem rewards, testing access, and community recognition.

Passes are minted on Solana by CBS holders. Benefits may evolve over time as the ecosystem grows — there are no guaranteed financial returns.

Built with CBS Tools · ManGo

ManGo is the first public project built and launched using the CBS toolchain. It is separate from CBS Coin itself — a real-world showcase of what the tools can do, not the core CBS project.

ManGo demonstrates how creators can go from idea to on-chain project using open, community-built infrastructure.
