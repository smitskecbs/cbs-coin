Project & Inspiration

I created CBS Coin because I believe in the power of community. It’s a meme with a serious message – not just about a token, but about building sovereignty, freedom, and value together without relying on banks or governments.
I’m building step by step, without rushing big updates. Since I’m still new to Web3, this is an experimental phase. The roadmap may look simple, but that’s intentional – it allows me to stay focused and actually achieve my goals.
I first got into Web3 years ago as an investor in Shiba Inu. I invested not only in SHIB, but also in the Leash and Bone tokens, and even bought a piece of land in the Shib Metaverse. I was impressed by how a so-called ‘meme coin’ with no utility could still gain such massive value. Later, when I started reading about Solana and how anyone could create their own token, the idea for CBS Coin was born. At first I minted it simply from my phone, but as people started asking questions, I got myself a laptop so I could add metadata and build properly. I’m still learning every day, improving step by step, and applying everything I learn to make the project better.
