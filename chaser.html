Holders-only (≥ 1,000 CBS). Move with WASD / Arrow keys or drag on mobile. Collect coins, avoid enemies.

How to play

Enemies chase you. Touching an enemy ends the run. Collect glowing CBS coins for +3 points each.

Keyboard: Arrow keys / WASD. Mobile: drag anywhere.
