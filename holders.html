How the count is calculated

The holder count is fetched via a CBS proxy that queries the Helius RPC for all token accounts of the CBS mint and then counts how many unique wallets actually hold a non-zero amount of CBS.

Different explorers may show slightly different numbers depending on dust filters, frozen accounts or how often they update. This page is meant as a transparent, on-chain view for the CBS community.
