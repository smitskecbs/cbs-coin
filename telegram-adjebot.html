Adje Telegram Bot

Official bot username: @Adjecatbot • Neutral, reusable, multi-group

What is Adje?

Adje is a clean, neutral Telegram bot you can use in any group or DM. It shows your project links, welcome/rules, does a daily /gm streak, a light auto‑chat (slash triggers only), and simple raid counters.

Bot handle: @Adjecatbot
Tip: In busy groups with other bots, mention Adje explicitly, e.g. /set@Adjecatbot website https://…

Quick Start (Admin)

  1. DM @Adjecatbot → send /start. If you get a reply, the bot is online.
  2. Add Adje to your group (you must be admin). Group privacy can stay ON.
  3. Set your links inside the group (or DM): 
    /set@Adjecatbot website https://example.com
/set@Adjecatbot x https://x.com/yourhandle
/set@Adjecatbot telegram https://t.me/yourgroup
/set@Adjecatbot mint https://solscan.io/token/YOUR_MINT
/set@Adjecatbot ca YOUR_SOLANA_MINT_ADDRESS   (base58, no https)
/set@Adjecatbot topic Layer-2, Airdrops, NFT, Staking
  4. Set texts: 
    /set@Adjecatbot welcome Welcome aboard! Check /links, read /rules, and say /gm to start your streak. Enjoy!
/set@Adjecatbot rules Be respectful | No scams/spam | No doxxing | No NSFW | DYOR | Mods’ decisions are final
  5. Test: 
    /links@Adjecatbot
/website@Adjecatbot   /x@Adjecatbot   /mint@Adjecatbot   /ca@Adjecatbot
/welcome@Adjecatbot   /rules@Adjecatbot   /topic@Adjecatbot

Troubleshooting

  • No response? Try in DM first: @Adjecatbot → /start. If it works in DM but not in your group, call the bot explicitly: /links@Adjecatbot.
  • Another bot grabs /set: use /aset or /set@Adjecatbot ….
  • Solana CA: it’s your mint address (base58, no https://).
  • Safety: Never share your seed phrase. Admins never DM first.
